Executive Overview

Venture partners needed an automated intelligence dashboard aggregating Stripe metrics, churn signals, customer NPS, and runway calculations across 45 portfolio companies with executive-ready weekly summaries.

The Architectural Challenge

Partners spent their entire weekends manually cross-referencing Stripe dashboards, Google Sheets, and Slack updates to construct weekly investor memorandums.

Our Engineered Solution

Integrated unified webhook ingestors, automated time-series anomaly detection algorithms, and Claude-powered markdown briefing generators with export to PDF and Notion.
